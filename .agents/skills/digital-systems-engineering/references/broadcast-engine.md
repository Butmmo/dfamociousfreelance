# The Broadcast Engine

Podcast production and editing. Core mechanism: genre-lock into one specific podcast category, plus a YouTube-repurposing gap most competing editors don't offer, as the entry wedge.

## The niche

Genre-locked, not open to any podcast — Business & Entrepreneurship is a named starting genre specifically because its hosts already understand marketing ROI and typically have real production budget, unlike genres where hosts are hobbyists first. Genre-lock also prevents internal competition between students who pick this same system.

## Pricing

Starter $500-700/month, Growth $800-1,200/month, Full Production $1,300-1,800/month.

## The 7-week arc

Learn the System & Build Editing Skills → Build Full Demo Portfolio → Pick Genre and Prospect → Outreach Launch → Close First Client → Deliver → Prove Value, Upsell, Referral.

## Proof strategy: practice episodes, never a real host's episode for free

Build the demo portfolio by editing practice episodes (self-sourced audio, not a real client's real show) — the same self-made-material pattern as every other system with a proof requirement in DSE. No correction needed here; this system was already built the right way.

## Scripts

Outreach message to a podcast host, a follow-up, discovery call questions, two objection handlers, an upsell pitch for existing clients, and a referral ask.

## Prospecting

Apple Podcasts and Spotify genre charts, plus LinkedIn posts where hosts promote their own show — visible, low-friction signals that a show is active and its host is invested enough to be worth approaching.
