# The D'Famocious Annual Honours

Two awards, both calendar-year, both drawing verification from infrastructure that already exists elsewhere in this ecosystem rather than requiring anything new to be built. This file has the fuller design; treat it as a strong first build, not a closed system — more categories can be added later without disrupting what's here.

## D'Famocious Influencer of the Year

**Basis:** the person with the most *verified* mentee DFY completions within the calendar year — not lifetime total, that year specifically.

**Why this is a real, ongoing incentive and not just a box to tick:** Leadership by Influence's baseline is 5 mentees for SUC eligibility (`references/leadership-by-influence.md`). This award has no ceiling and no stopping point at 5 — someone who's already SUC-eligible, or already running a SUC business, can keep mentoring and keep competing for this. That's the actual point: it turns mentorship from a one-time qualification hurdle into something worth continuing indefinitely, which is exactly what an Alumni/Mentorship tier should do.

**Verification:** no new system needed. The count comes directly from the same two-sided-attestation, Rep-verified completions Leadership by Influence already tracks. A live leaderboard is just a rollup of existing data by calendar year.

**Eligibility safeguard:** zero Level 3 or Level 4 offences in the qualifying year (`bef`'s `references/identity-and-framework.md`) — consistent with how ordinary advancement already works elsewhere in this ecosystem. Don't recognize someone as the year's standard-bearer while they're carrying an unresolved serious offence.

**Reward:** two company-hosted conferences that year, where the recipient trains participants on their own journey — a real platform, not just a title. Plus a **$1,500 cash prize**, funded by BEF (this is a BEF/NBO-side achievement) — anchored near the existing $1,000 BEF-DBI per-intern reimbursement but scaled up to reflect a competitive annual honor, not a routine payment. Review this figure periodically as the organization grows rather than treating it as permanently fixed — the Board of Stewards is the natural body to revisit it once it exists.

**The cash component holds until DBI reaches 100 lifetime DFY graduates — the title, conferences, and recognition itself do not wait for this.** A real institutional-capacity gate, not a downgrade of the award: the honor can be given meaningfully at any scale, cash commitments shouldn't be made before the organization can sustain them without strain. Read as a one-time, cumulative threshold (100 total graduates, ever, not per-year or concurrently-active) — once crossed, it stays crossed. Applies forward from the point it's reached; past award cycles before the threshold don't get retroactive cash.

**Tie-breaking, three layers deep:**
1. First to qualify within the year wins outright.
2. If that's still a literal tie (same confirmed qualification moment), the tiebreaker is **total lifetime verified mentee completions**, not just the current year's — whoever has mentored more people to completion across their whole tenure wins.
3. If that also ties — vanishingly unlikely, since it requires identical lifetime totals *and* identical qualification timestamps — the award and all its perks are shared between the tied recipients.

This is rarer than it sounds in practice: completions are confirmed through periodic Rep review of accountability sheets, not continuous real-time tracking, so "exact date and time" is already a soft concept today, and gets more precise (not less) once the planned Stripe system replaces manual reporting.

## D'Famocious Innovator of the Year

**Basis:** the SUC-stage founder with the highest *audited* net profit by year's end. Audited specifically, not self-reported — the same rigor already required of Verified Retail and DFY reporting should apply here, verified through the Auditor role (`references/rep-and-auditor.md`).

**Eligibility safeguard:** same as above — zero Level 3/4 offences in the qualifying year. High profit achieved through conduct that would otherwise draw discipline shouldn't be the year's celebrated example.

**Reward:** a special seat on the Council of Elders for that year, plus the same conference-speaking platform as the Influencer of the Year, plus a **$500 cash prize** — deliberately modest and ceremonial, since the recipient is by definition already the year's highest earner; the seat and platform carry the actual weight here. Funded by DBI (this is a SUC-side, equity-bearing achievement), not BEF. Same activation gate as the Influencer prize above: holds until DBI reaches 100 lifetime DFY graduates; the seat and platform don't wait for it.

### The Elder seat needs scoping, and here's my actual recommendation, not just the question

The Elder Council's sole, non-delegable mandate is doctrinal defense of Core Principles and Catholic Identity (`dfg`'s `SKILL.md`) — a fundamentally different competency than business profitability. A brilliant, profitable founder isn't automatically suited to doctrinal guardianship, and conflating the two risks diluting what the Elder Council is actually for.

**My recommendation: full presence, scoped voting.** The Innovator of the Year should sit with the Council for that year, be present at proceedings, and have real voice — but I'd exclude them specifically from the two-thirds Doctrinal Nullity Declaration vote (the Council's core, defining power), reserving that to Elders vetted and tenured for doctrinal judgment specifically. This keeps the honor genuinely prestigious and meaningful without treating one year of profitability as equivalent to the "proven fidelity, leadership maturity, and deep understanding of BEF systems" standing Elders are actually selected for. If full voting rights feel important to the honor's weight, that's a real, legitimate alternative view — but I'd want that chosen deliberately, not by default.

## Naming and timing

Announce both once the calendar year's results are final; the conferences and (for the Innovator) the Elder seat run from that point, whether that lands late in the same year or opens into the next depending on scheduling. A third category — recognizing strong DSE-stage performance itself, before SUC and before heavy mentorship — would be a natural future addition if you want a rung between "just started" and "already mentoring or already founded a business," but that's not built here; flagging it as available, not designing it now.
