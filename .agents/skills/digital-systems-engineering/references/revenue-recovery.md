# Revenue Recovery Engine

Ecommerce email/SMS retention via Klaviyo. Core flows: Abandoned Cart, Welcome, Post-Purchase, Win-Back. No free-work correction needed here — proof is built on a self-made dummy store, never given away to a real prospect for free.

## The niche

Ecommerce brands, scored by segment — Skincare & Beauty and Supplements & Wellness are the named starting niches, chosen for high repeat-purchase behavior (which is exactly what these flows are built to capture) and active, visible marketing presence (making prospecting straightforward via Instagram, TikTok, and the Meta Ad Library).

## Pricing

Starter $297-397/month, Growth $500-700/month, Full Revenue System $800-1,200/month.

## The 7-week arc

Learn the System → Build Full Demo Portfolio → Pick Niche and Prospect → Outreach Launch → Close and Begin Delivery → Launch and Prove Value → Upsell, Referral, and Scale.

## Proof strategy: the dummy store, done right

Build a free Klaviyo account (free tier supports this) and a dummy Shopify store, then build out all four core flows against it as the actual portfolio piece — this is the same self-made-material pattern as Ascent's mock call or Broadcast's practice episodes, and it's fine exactly because no real store owner is involved. Be upfront that it's a demo, not real client results; the flows themselves, shown live, do the persuading.

## Scripts

Instagram DM opener, cold email built around a specific identified gap in the prospect's current flows, a follow-up message, discovery call questions, two objection handlers, an SMS upsell pitch for existing clients, and a referral ask.

## Prospecting

Meta Ad Library and active Instagram/TikTok presence are the primary sourcing signal — a brand spending on ads has budget and is actively trying to convert traffic, which is exactly the audience these flows serve.
