# CareBridge

Home care agency automation. Core mechanism: a mystery-shopper audit (call each target agency as a genuine inquiry, time their response) generates the exact proof needed to open a conversation — this is real market research, not free work, and stays as-is.

## The niche

Home care agencies and assisted living facilities, scored Hot/Warm/Cold by response-time gap after the mystery-shopper call — outreach focuses only on Hot and Warm targets, not the full researched list.

## Pricing

Foundation $75-150/month, Growth $175-350/month, Full Care System $350-600/month.

## The 7-week arc

Learn the System & Build Foundation → Build Full Demo System → Identify Local Agencies (mystery-shop 15-20, score, prioritize) → Warm Approach → Close and Pilot → Deliver and Prove Value → Convert, Upsell, Referral.

## A correction to the source system's pilot mechanic

The underlying system's script offers a fully free pilot ("set up the system for your next 5 inquiries, completely free, no commitment, no cost") to cold, researched prospects — agencies the student found and scored, not existing contacts. That's a real instance of the pattern this skill doesn't advise: free work handed to a cold prospect trains them to expect free work, and undercuts the paid close it's meant to earn.

**Corrected approach, same underlying logic, different mechanism:** for a cold, researched agency, offer a **low-cost, guarantee-backed pilot** instead of a free one — e.g., a 30-day trial at a small fee (roughly $75-100), fully refunded if the agency doesn't see a measurable drop in response time. This keeps the actual persuasive engine intact (zero real financial risk, proof before a bigger ask) without the free-work downside — the prospect has genuine skin in the game, which is also what makes the eventual "convert to retainer" conversation credible rather than feeling like a bait-and-switch.

**The one case where a genuinely free pilot is still fine:** an agency that came through a warm referral from an existing client (the referral-ask at the end of this same cycle). Trust already exists there in a way it doesn't with a cold, researched target — treat referred prospects and cold-researched prospects differently, not with one blanket rule.

Everything downstream stays the same either way: present results with the specific before/after number, anchor the paid retainer price directly to what the pilot just demonstrated, then ask for a referral.

## Objection handling

"We already have a system" → most care software handles scheduling/documentation, rarely instant family communication specifically; offer to audit their current setup and report honestly either way. "No budget" → this is where the guarantee replaces "it's free" as the risk-reversal — lead with the refund guarantee, not with zero cost.
