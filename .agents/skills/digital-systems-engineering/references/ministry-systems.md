# Digital Ministry Systems

Church/parish management systems (ChMS) for congregations mainstream ChMS platforms don't design for — particularly African, Filipino, and Latino immigrant congregations. Warm-network, relationship-first sales, not cold outreach.

## Pricing

Foundation $100-200/month, Growth $250-450/month, Full Ministry $500-900/month.

## The 9-part arc

Audit Your Own Ministry → Launch the Pilot → Document the Case Study → Identify Your Warm Network → Present and Close → Deliver → Support and Prove Value → Expand the Relationship → Review and Plan to Scale.

## Already structured the way the free-work principle asks for — worth noting explicitly, not just leaving implicit

Unlike CareBridge, this system's pilot doesn't need correcting. It starts on the student's **own** parish (Week 1's audit, Week 2's pilot) — not a cold prospect, an existing community the student already belongs to. Expansion beyond that runs explicitly through "warm network" (Week 4's title) and referrals within it, not researched cold outreach the way CareBridge's agency list is. A free or low-cost pilot on a warm, already-trusted contact is exactly the exception this skill's cross-cutting rule allows for — apply the system as designed here, unlike CareBridge.

## Scripts

Bringing the system up with the student's own church leadership; presenting pilot results (specific before/after, e.g., event registration or communication response improvements); introducing the system to a new parish or ministry (framed through the warm-network relationship, not cold introduction); two objection handlers for common leadership pushback; asking for a referral within the broader network the student's own church connects to (diocese, ministerial association, sister parishes).

## Why this niche specifically

Mainstream ChMS platforms are built for large, English-first, already-formalized congregations — immigrant and smaller congregations are underserved by design, not by accident, which is exactly the gap this system is built to fill. The student's own lived connection to a specific community (language, culture, denomination) is often the actual differentiator, not the software itself.
