# The Authority Engine

LinkedIn ghostwriting. Core mechanism: a structured voice-capture interview, then consistent, on-schedule posting in the client's authentic voice — the value isn't the writing itself so much as the interview process, the judgment to sound like the client rather than generic AI output, and simply showing up on schedule.

## The niche: ten ICP personas

Real estate agents, wellness coaches, SaaS founders, financial advisors, executive coaches, recruiters, fractional CMOs, attorneys, VCs, and authors/speakers — all people who benefit from a visible LinkedIn presence but don't have time to write it themselves, the same underlying reason SMB owners don't build their own websites.

Build Your Writing Foundation → Build Your Portfolio → Identify and Engage → [proof/conversion step, corrected below] → Close Your First Client → Deliver → Prove Value, Upsell, Referral.

## A correction to the source system's conversion mechanic

The underlying system's core conversion move is "The Free Sample Approach" — writing a brand-new post, from scratch, specifically for a real, cold prospect, for free, as the way to prove skill and open the sale. That's a real instance of the pattern this skill doesn't advise: free work handed to a cold prospect trains them to expect free work, even when it's a single post rather than an ongoing service.

**Corrected approach, same underlying goal, different mechanism:** lead with the **dummy portfolio** instead — posts already written for a fictional persona, which the source system's own FAQ correctly treats as legitimate free practice, since no real prospect is involved in making them. Show 2-3 strong portfolio pieces as the primary proof of writing skill; this costs the prospect nothing to evaluate and costs the student no new unpaid labor per prospect. For a genuinely interested prospect who specifically wants to see their *own* voice captured before committing — a fair ask, since a fictional-persona sample doesn't prove voice-matching for them specifically — offer a **small paid diagnostic post** (roughly $25-50) instead of a free one, credited in full toward the first month if they move forward. This preserves the real value (seeing their actual voice on the page) without training them to expect free work as the default.

## Scripts

An opening DM built around the portfolio rather than a free sample offer, a follow-up, the voice-capture questionnaire (the actual product-quality driver), a discovery call that walks from portfolio interest to the paid diagnostic to a full retainer, two objection handlers, and an upsell pitch for existing clients.

## Prospecting

Identify and engage prospects through their existing LinkedIn activity — people already posting inconsistently or not at all despite clear expertise are the strongest signal, since the gap between what they know and what they're visibly sharing is the whole pitch.
