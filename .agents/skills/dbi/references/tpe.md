# The Practice of Enterprise (TPE)

WOM's actual successor — not just "fulfilled through the DBI app" in the abstract (as previously noted), but a real, structured curriculum. Runs across all of DBI: every DSE and SUC participant, not NBO-specific, consistent with how BPS Cadence became universal.

## The twelve topics, in six movements — not twelve unrelated lessons

Order, Intelligence, Leadership & Vision, Communication, Time & Effort, Strategy & Innovation, Execution & Discipline, Systems-Building, Adaptability & Resilience, Financial Intelligence, Culture & Team Building, Sustainability & Ethics (all "in Enterprise"). These group into six movements of two-to-three topics each, each answering one underlying question, building one continuous arc rather than twelve standalone videos — full curriculum, the per-topic content, and the reasoning for the sequence: `references/tpe-curriculum.md`.

Twelve weeks approximates one quarter — the cycle doesn't end after one pass. Week 13 returns to Topic 1 and the cycle repeats for as long as someone remains a DBI participant. This is deliberate: these read as durable principles worth revisiting, not a course to finish once and move past — an "Order in Enterprise" video watched in month 2 and again in month 14 lands differently once real experience sits behind it. The closing movement (Sustainability & Ethics) is the hinge back to the opening one (Order) on purpose, not a coincidence of the numbering.

## Mechanics

- **Fixed to Monday**, matching both the natural start of the week and BPS Cadence's own weekly reporting rhythm.
- **Format:** a pre-recorded video per topic.
- **Requirement:** at least one interaction with that week's video, once per week. The app marks the activity complete on interaction — binary, consistent with how every other tracking mechanism in this ecosystem works. This binary mark is untouched by the signal layer below — it fires the same way regardless of anything that happens after it.
- **A missed week is tracked, not punished.** Consistent with the support-not-punishment posture already established for DFY — a missed TPE week is visible data, not grounds for a sanction. If someone accumulates a real pattern of missed weeks, that's a support conversation (via their Rep or mentor), not an automatic consequence.

## Worth deliberately aligning, not leaving as two separate obligations

TPE's Monday requirement and BPS Cadence's weekly reporting cadence (`dfg`'s `references/bps-cadence.md`) can be the same weekly touchpoint rather than two unrelated ones — a participant's Monday could naturally include both their weekly BPS report and that week's TPE video in one sitting. This isn't confirmed policy, just a real design opportunity worth taking rather than defaulting into two disconnected weekly asks landing on different days. This same alignment is what the signal layer below builds on directly, rather than creating a new place to check.

## Beyond binary: does it actually land?

Binary completion tells you someone opened the video, not whether it did anything. A genuinely well-designed, opt-in signal layer sits on top of the binary tracking above — a single-tap reaction at the moment of completion, and an optional line folded into the existing BPS report — without replacing, gating, or scoring against the binary mechanic. Full system, including the real discipline around *why* this doesn't drift into a surveillance layer over time: `references/tpe-signal-layer.md`.

## What's still genuinely open

Two things the curriculum and signal layer together don't fully resolve:

- **Whether video content itself gets refreshed** now has a real signal to decide with — if "Didn't land this time" clusters heavily on one topic across cycles (`references/tpe-signal-layer.md`), that's a direct, content-level answer. But whether refreshing actually happens, and on what cadence, isn't decided.
- **A script or prompt set for the Rep/mentor support conversation** when someone accumulates missed weeks isn't built yet. A reasonable starting shape: open with the participant's own past Landing Check notes where available (already permitted, see the signal layer's data-access rules), frame the conversation using the Acceptable/Correctable/Disciplinary classification already governing everything else in this ecosystem (`bef`'s `references/identity-and-framework.md`) rather than assuming missed weeks are misconduct, and keep it explicitly about removing whatever's actually in the way, not documenting the absence. This is my own proposed shape, not confirmed policy — the tone and specifics are worth the user's own judgment, not mine.
