# Next Blazers Organisation (NBO)

BEF's (Blazer Empowerment Foundation) first named initiative — and in practice its operating arm. BEF doesn't run programs directly; it holds the mandate, identity, and values, while NBO administers nearly everything participant-facing: training, participant status and progression, discipline, and income-discipline mechanisms.

**Working reference: NBO Constitution (Restated).** This skill implements and elaborates that document operationally; where the two differ, the Constitution governs.

NBO's entry-level function is subsidizing DSE — reducing the $900 entry to $90 (see `dbi` and `digital-systems-engineering` for the full DSE pricing context this sits inside). Grows primarily through word-of-mouth invitation — relationship and connection with the person who invites someone matters as much as the offer itself — though the organization or individual members can also use more intentional channels (ads, etc.). Three systems: Next Blazers College (NBC), Next Blazers Scholarship (NBS), Blazer People Network (BPN).

## Next Blazers College (NBC)

An intensive prep program preceding DSE — **58 days core** (approximately two months), plus a 30-day self-development reading tail that extends past it.

**Entry:** an interested person files a form on the website — their own contact information, their informant's (future sponsor's) information, and their reasons/intentions for joining. After review, if approved, an invitation email is sent to the NBO app.

**Five stages:** Onboarding (10 days) → Blazer Productivity Scheme Training (10 days) → BEF Exposition Course, "Who We Are" (7 days) → Sales and Marketing (21 days) → DBI Introductory Course (10 days). Full curriculum detail — books, assignments, and the Three Goals System that anchors BPS — in `references/nbc-curriculum.md`.

**Pace enforcement:** cohort-first, with a capped fee as the specific consequence for missing a cohort's window. Fixed-date cohorts with a shared digital group space and peer accountability are the everyday mechanism — falling behind triggers a sponsor check-in, not a charge. Miss the full 60-day window entirely, though, and the choice is real: lose participant status, or pay $25/month for one make-up cohort (capped at one use, not indefinitely payable). Full mechanism and reasoning: `references/nbc-curriculum.md`.

**Titling:** stages 1-3 = **NBR (Next Blazers Recruit)**. Stages 4-5 = **Intern**. A member stays an Intern until they sign up for Blazer People Network — at that point they become a **Next Blazers Beneficiary (NBB)**. On completing the DBI Introductory Course, the Intern is invited into Next Blazers Scholarship and progression continues; they don't wait for the 30-day reading tail to finish.

## Next Blazers Scholarship (NBS)

The internal name for the 90% discount NBO attaches to DSE — $900 down to $90. Not a separate program in its own right; it's the mechanism name for what's elsewhere just called "the NBO subsidy."

## Blazer People Network (BPN)

How networking capacity turns into an income stream, in partnership with Neolife International. **Open to any DSE participant, not limited to NBO's subsidized track** — formerly Next Blazers Network (NBN), renamed and opened up because there was no principled reason to reserve a well-designed opportunity for the population least equipped to absorb a bad outcome from it.

**The emphasis is personal use, not active selling.** Members are encouraged to buy enough product for themselves and their family to use, rather than sell aggressively — active selling isn't prohibited, but it isn't required either. Part of this is a genuine health angle: Neolife's food supplement line specifically, positioned around promoting healthy living among beneficiaries, not just as an income mechanism.

**PPV cap:** personal purchases (not counting anything sold to customers) must stay under 300 PPV, advised to stay closer to 250 PPV — framed as the better structure for building a network over time, not just a compliance ceiling.

**Eligibility to join BPN:** a beneficiary needs at least $1,000 in personal net income earned since starting DSE, before they can participate — a financial-readiness gate, since BPN itself requires ongoing product spend.

**Funding the product purchases:** BEF's Pocket System — mandatory segregation of personal income into defined categories including a dedicated "PV Use" category — is the purpose-built mechanism for this, and should be taught as such rather than leaving beneficiaries to improvise from general income. See `bef`'s `references/identity-and-framework.md`.

**Sponsorship:** a beneficiary signs up through whoever invited/informed them about NBO — that person becomes their Neolife sponsor. If someone finds NBO independently (through BEF directly, the website on their own, or walking into a DBI facility without a specific informant), they sign up under the Founder's own Neolife sponsorship (Boluwatife Famokunwa) instead of an orphaned line.

**BPN and SUC eligibility:** BPN participation offers real, relevant people-management practice, but SUC eligibility no longer runs through any Neolife rank. It runs through DBI's own Leadership by Influence standard instead — 5 people personally mentored through their own full DFY completion, or (for candidates active in BPN specifically) verified retail sales. Full detail and the reasoning for why the earlier Sapphire Director requirement was removed: `digital-systems-engineering`'s `references/leadership-by-influence.md`.

## Training governance roles

NBC runs on a three-office separation inherited from BEF's constitution: NBA (command and policy), NBS (records and traceability), NBX (tactical execution and mentorship, explicitly non-policy-setting). **The NBO sponsor and NBX are the same function under two names** — the sponsor is the participant's NBX, and inherits its boundaries. NBS's clerical mandate is largely automatable into DBI's own app, but any automated sanction still needs a named human in the appeal path, or the inherited fair-warning principle breaks. Full detail, including where the employment/contractor line falls: `references/training-governance-roles.md`.

## BEF institutional identity

NBO operates under BEF's mandate, not as a self-contained institution — the B.L.A.Z.E.R. values, the Formal Covenant, independent-contractor status, the Pocket System, the discipline ladder, Catholic identity, and The Panel all belong to BEF itself, not to NBO specifically. Full detail → load `bef`.

## Worth flagging directly, not just documenting

BPN integrates a real, external network-marketing company into a training pipeline that reaches financially subsidized participants specifically — even opened up universally now, that population is still present within it. Worth staying deliberate that "active selling isn't required" holds true in practice, not just on paper, and that any income-potential framing given to beneficiaries stays honest rather than aspirational. This isn't a reason to avoid the structure — the personal-use focus, the absence of a selling requirement, and the genuine health rationale for the product line already read as more conservative than typical MLM onboarding — but it's the kind of real-world detail worth the same review the equity and DFY structures already got.
